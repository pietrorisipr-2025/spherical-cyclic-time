# Replacement section EN - Auxiliary centerless timeline-bundle visualization

The original SCT visualization used an auxiliary circular/spherical picture to organize families of possible temporal lines. In the v2.2 clarification, this picture is retained only as a **nonphysical centerless timeline-bundle visualization**.

The common marker in the drawing is not a spatial point, not a spacetime location, and not a center of the universe. It denotes the common Big-Bang boundary condition from which the modeled temporal lines are treated as emerging.

A minimal notation is:

```text
B(sigma) = { L_i(sigma) }
```

where `L_i` is a modeled temporal line and `sigma` is a nonphysical unfolding, phase, accessibility, capacity, or model-dependent scale parameter. A circular or spherical diagram may be used to draw layers of `B(sigma)`, but those layers are not material shells and do not represent a front of cosmic expansion.

If the legacy great-circle construction is kept for historical continuity, it should be introduced as a diagrammatic convention only. Any common point `p` in that construction is a graphical boundary marker, not a spacetime event and not a physical center.

Recommended caption:

> Centerless timeline-bundle visualization. The graphical marker denotes the common Big-Bang boundary condition for the modeled temporal lines. It is not a spatial center, and the circular layers are not physical expansion shells.
