# Long public note - SCT v2.2-E

SCT is best read as an exploratory model of temporal-line organization, not as a direct claim about the literal shape of the physical universe.

The phrase "Spherical Cyclic Time" is historical and diagrammatic. The spherical/circular language is used to visualize a centerless bundle of modeled temporal lines that share a common Big-Bang boundary condition. It does not mean that the Big Bang is a point at the center of an already-existing space, that the universe expands from one side, or that the physical universe is asserted to be a material sphere.

The safe public interpretation is:

1. The Big Bang marker is a common temporal boundary condition, not a spatial center.
2. Modeled temporal lines may be drawn as circular/spherical layers for visualization only.
3. Radius-like coordinates represent unfolding, phase, accessibility, capacity, or model-dependent scale.
4. Transfer-kernel diagnostics remain model-dependent and do not by themselves constitute a cosmological observation or prediction.
5. Any physical mapping must be separately declared, justified, and validated.
