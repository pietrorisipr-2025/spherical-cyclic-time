# Zenodo description block - SCT v2.2-E

Spherical Cyclic Time (SCT) is an exploratory, non-peer-reviewed, toy/informational framework for cyclic phase, unwrapped time, relative restart, accessible informational capacity, and model-dependent transfer-kernel diagnostics.

## v2.2 clarification: centerless timeline-bundle wording

In the v2.2 clarification, SCT circular/spherical diagrams are not presented as a literal physical geometry of the universe. They are nonphysical visualizations of a centerless bundle of temporal lines born from a common Big-Bang boundary condition.

The graphical origin in these drawings is not a spatial center, not a location in spacetime, and not a privileged edge of the universe. The displayed circular or spherical layers are not material shells and do not represent a front of cosmic expansion. Radius-like quantities in SCT denote phase, unfolding, accessibility, capacity, or a model-dependent scale unless an explicit toy-model mapping states otherwise.

This wording patch does not change the v2.0/v2.1 transfer-kernel results. It adds a safer public interpretation layer to avoid confusing the timeline-bundle visualization with a physical cosmological claim.

## Claim boundary

This release does not claim:

- a complete physical cosmology;
- an observational prediction;
- a measured cosmological parameter;
- a universal transfer kernel;
- a spatial Big-Bang center;
- a literal spherical universe;
- that all modeled temporal lines physically exist.

It preserves the original claim boundary: SCT remains an exploratory/toy/informational framework unless and until a separate physical model, observational mapping, and validation program are explicitly supplied.
