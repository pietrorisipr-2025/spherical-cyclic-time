# README notice block - SCT v2.2-E

## Public terminology notice: timeline-bundle, not physical universe geometry

From the v2.2 clarification onward, SCT uses the expression **centerless timeline-bundle visualization** for its circular/spherical diagrams.

This means:

- the Big Bang is treated here as a common boundary condition for modeled temporal lines, not as a spatial center;
- the graphical point in a diagram is only a marker, not a physical event-location inside a pre-existing space;
- the drawn circular/spherical layers are levels of unfolding, phase, accessibility, capacity, or model-dependent scale;
- the drawings are not material shells, not an expansion front, and not a claim that the physical universe is a literal sphere;
- the v2.0/v2.1 transfer-kernel results remain unchanged.

Recommended one-line summary:

> SCT models a centerless bundle of temporal lines born from a common Big-Bang boundary condition; circular/spherical drawings are nonphysical visualization tools, not a claim of a spatial center or literal spherical universe.
