# SCT v2.2-E - Integrated Public Mini-Release: Centerless Timeline-Bundle Wording

Date: 2026-07-08

## Release decision

`SCT_V2_2_E_INTEGRATED_PUBLIC_MINI_RELEASE_TIMELINE_BUNDLE_DONE`

This mini-release integrates the v2.2-B, v2.2-C, and v2.2-D clarification path into a compact public addendum. It is meant to sit beside the public SCT v2.1-H / v2.1.0 material as a semantic and presentation correction.

## What changed

The object of the clarification is not the physical geometry of the universe. The object is the visualization of the bundle of modeled temporal lines born from a common Big-Bang boundary condition.

The safe wording is:

> SCT models or visualizes a centerless bundle of temporal lines born from a common Big-Bang boundary condition. Circular or spherical drawings are nonphysical visualization tools; they are not a claim of a spatial center, a privileged edge, a material shell structure, or a literal spherical universe.

## What did not change

- No numerical execution was performed.
- No transfer-kernel result from v2.0/v2.1 was changed.
- No observational claim was added.
- No claim of complete physical cosmology was added.
- No claim of a spatial Big-Bang center was added.
- No claim that all modeled temporal lines physically exist was added.
