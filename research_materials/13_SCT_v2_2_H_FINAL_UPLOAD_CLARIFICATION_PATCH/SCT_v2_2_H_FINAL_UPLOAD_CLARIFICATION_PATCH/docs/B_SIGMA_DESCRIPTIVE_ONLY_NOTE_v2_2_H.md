# B(sigma) notation note - v2.2-H

The notation

`B(sigma) = {L_i(sigma)}`

is descriptive only. It denotes a schematic collection of modeled timeline states at an abstract bundle parameter sigma. It is not introduced as:

- a new dynamical equation,
- a new physical field,
- a new computational input,
- a claim of physical existence for all modeled temporal lines,
- or a replacement for the existing v2.0/v2.1 transfer-kernel formalism.

Its purpose is only to prevent the older auxiliary-sphere wording from being misread as a literal spatial center, material shell, physical boundary, or universe-scale expansion geometry.
