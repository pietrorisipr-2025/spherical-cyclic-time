# Close-or-Continue Decision

## Conservative decision

Close the v1.4 technical branch for now at the level of minimal reproducibility.

## Rationale

The branch has produced useful structure and negative results, but the remaining open items are not small implementation details. They require independent physical input:

- `Gamma_S` requires a microphysical or fluid model.
- `P_xi(k)`, `k_c`, and `p` require an independently specified regeneration mechanism.
- `S_cap` requires a covariant light-sheet or causal-diamond upgrade before being treated as more than FLRW/Hubble diagnostic capacity.

## Continue only if

A future step supplies an independent derivation/source, not a parameter choice selected by toy success.

## Publication readiness

If the goal is publication/archive, the next step is release preparation from v1.3-I plus v1.4-F reproducibility notes, not new physics claims.
