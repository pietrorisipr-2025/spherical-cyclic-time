# Claim Boundary — SCT v1.4-F

Allowed:

- SCT is a toy/informational grammar for relative restart under a monotone absolute entropy floor.
- v1.4-F makes the current toy diagnostic branch minimally reproducible.
- The v1.3-B and v1.3-C scans contain both favorable toy regions and explicit failures.
- The coherent perturbative region is small relative to the tested grid and depends on non-derived parameters.

Not allowed:

- A physical cyclic cosmology has been derived.
- A microphysical entropy-production rate `Gamma_S` has been derived.
- A physical regeneration spectrum `P_xi(k)` has been derived.
- The causal-patch capacity is a full covariant derivation of accessible states.
- Any CMB/LSS/observational signature is predicted.
- Parameters are selected because they produce successful restarts.
