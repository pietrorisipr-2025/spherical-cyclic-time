# Claim Boundary — SCT v2.1-H

Allowed public claims:

1. SCT remains a toy/informational framework with no physical cosmology or observational claim.
2. v1.7–v1.9 are requirements/survey obstruction audits, not computational negative results.
3. v2.0 contains two preregistered minimal closed-background transfer tests.
4. Both tested toy background families produced non-gaussian, bounded/high-k plateau-like transfer shapes under the locked rules.
5. v2.1 records an analytic explanation of the plateau mechanism for the closed deterministic proxy route.
6. The conservative mainline is now `T_R(k) = model-dependent transfer operator`.
7. The gaussian kernel remains only a conditional open-system/diffusive toy limit.
8. The plateau-like kernel remains only a closed deterministic proxy toy branch.

Forbidden public claims:

1. No claim of a complete physical cyclic cosmology.
2. No observational prediction or fit.
3. No no-go theorem for cyclic or bouncing backgrounds.
4. No universal transfer kernel.
5. No derivation of `P_xi`, `Gamma_S`, FDT/KMS, bath physics, entropy map, or restart dynamics.
6. No claim that SCT as an informational framework is falsified.
7. No claim that gaussian damping is impossible in an independently specified open-system/diffusive model.
