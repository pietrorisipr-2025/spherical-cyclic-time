## Description draft

This deposit presents version 2.1.0 of Spherical Cyclic Time (SCT), an exploratory, non-peer-reviewed toy/informational framework for cyclic phase, unwrapped time, relative entropy restart, accessible informational capacity, and transfer-kernel diagnostics.

The update should be read as a methodological and computational correction to the earlier public version. The v1.7–v1.9 line is classified as a requirements/survey obstruction audit rather than a computational negative result. The v2.0 branch refocused the project onto a minimal preregistered transfer question: whether simple closed-background toy transitions produce a gaussian/diffusive transfer shape. Two preregistered toy background families were executed under locked rules; both produced non-gaussian, bounded/high-k plateau-like transfer shapes.

The v2.1 patch updates the conservative mainline transfer language. The transfer is now treated as a model-dependent operator, `T_R(k)`, rather than as a gaussian default for closed deterministic backgrounds. The gaussian/diffusive form remains allowed only as a conditional open-system/diffusive toy limit; the plateau-like form is recorded only as a closed deterministic proxy toy branch.

The work does not claim to provide a complete physical cosmology, a new theory of gravity, an observational prediction, a no-go theorem for cyclic/bounce cosmologies, a universal transfer kernel, or a derivation of bath/noise/FDT/entropy-map/restart dynamics. It is not peer reviewed.
